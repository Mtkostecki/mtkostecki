Hello!

My name is Mateusz Kostecki and I am a postdoc at Gaspar Jekely's lab in Heidelberg.

I work on Placozoans, simple, nerveless animals. I am interested how do they coordinate their foraging behaviour using chemical signalling, with forays into their social behaviours.

I am also the founder and coordinator of Open Lab project, running since 2018.
